Private Client Services

For clients who prefer one trusted point of contact for personal, property, family and business administration in Thailand — with discretion built into every engagement.

A single relationship

Private Client engagements are designed for executives, families and high-value individuals who want continuity, discretion and clear accountability rather than a series of one-off transactions.

Your AVA case owner becomes the organising centre for the work that matters — coordinating professionals, tracking deadlines, managing local administration and reporting progress in plain language. Pricing is provided privately after scope is understood.

Service focus

What Private Client covers

Each service is structured around special operating care and a defined privacy standard. Fees are confirmed in a private quotation.

Executive Concierge

A dedicated private-office channel for appointments, government matters, personal administration and selected executive tasks — one trusted contact instead of fragmented vendors.

Privacy. Task lists and personal details stay within the assigned case channel. Third parties receive only the minimum information required to complete an authorised request. No marketing use of client identity or itinerary.

Executive Turnkey Solution

Multi-workstream project management under one mandate — immigration, residence, family, banking, property and professional coordination — with structured reporting rather than ad-hoc messages.

Privacy. Sensitive family, corporate and financial context is shared with external advisers only under your instruction. Status reports are issued through an agreed private channel; public or social reference to the engagement is not made.

Corporate Integrity Retainer

Ongoing compliance calendar, expiry tracking, document register and administrative risk review so corporate deadlines do not depend on informal memory.

Privacy. Corporate records and director information are handled as confidential working files. Access is limited to the engagement team and professionals you authorise. Reporting is management-facing only.

Property Transaction Coordination

Administrative control from document collection and Land Office preparation through transfer-day support and a clean handover file — without AVA acting as broker or legal counsel.

Privacy. Title, price and party details are shared only with parties you designate (lawyer, counterparty, agent). Transaction files are retained for administrative continuity, not for marketing or unrelated introductions.

Independent Professional Coordination

Client-side coordination across lawyers, accountants, tax advisers and specialists so documents, deadlines and decisions stay aligned — without AVA replacing professional judgement.

Privacy. AVA communicates with your appointed professionals only under your mandate. Matter content is not discussed with unrelated third parties. Consolidated status updates remain within the authorised circle.

Thailand Trip Design

Bespoke itinerary, logistics and concierge-style support built around your schedule and preferences — not a standard package tour.

Privacy. Travel preferences, companion names and schedules are used only to arrange the agreed trip. Vendors receive only booking-necessary details. AVA does not publish client travel stories or itineraries.

Discretion standard

How privacy works across Private Client work

  • Named case owner. One primary contact holds context so you are not required to restate sensitive details to every vendor.
  • Minimum necessary disclosure. Banks, schools, agents and authorities receive only what the process requires — not your full private file.
  • No public association. AVA does not use Private Client names, logos or circumstances in marketing, case studies or social media.
  • PDPA-aligned handling. Personal data is processed for the agreed engagement under Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Written scope. Engagement starts with a clear Scope of Work so privacy expectations, channels and authorised parties are explicit.

Detailed package descriptions and commercial terms are available on the Services page or on request. Private Client pricing is quoted individually.

Engagement model

How Private Client work is structured

Every engagement begins with a written scope of work, a defined communication channel and a named case owner. Additional project work is quoted separately with change-control so scope does not expand without agreement.
