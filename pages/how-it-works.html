Process

How AVA Works

A disciplined sequence from first conversation to completed deliverables — so you always know what is happening and who is responsible.

01 — DISCOVER

You share your objective, timeline and constraints. We clarify the practical questions and determine whether AVA is the right fit for the work required.

02 — ASSESS

Structured review of status, documents and known requirements. For many clients this begins with a Thailand Path Assessment or a Corporate Integrity Review.

03 — PLAN

Written Scope of Work, fee schedule, exclusions, client responsibilities and named case owner. Work begins only after written acceptance and the agreed deposit.

04 — COORDINATE

Execution of the agreed scope: document preparation, professional liaison, appointments, field representation and progress reporting. Material scope changes are confirmed in writing before additional work proceeds.

05 — COMPLETE

Delivery of agreed outputs, receipts and a closure pack including unresolved items, expiry dates and recommended next actions. Pending government decisions are not treated as AVA failures unless the agreed scope has been breached.
