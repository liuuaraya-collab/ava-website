Aviation Executive Services

Specialist administrative support for aircraft owners, pilots and aviation operators in Thailand.

Administrative precision for aviation matters

Aviation work in Thailand involves multiple authorities, document chains and renewal cycles. AVA provides structured administrative coordination so that owners, operators and pilots have a clear record, tracked deadlines and organised liaison with relevant parties.

We do not provide aviation legal opinions, engineering certification or flight operations decisions. Where specialist advice is required, we coordinate with appropriately qualified professionals.
