Aviation Requires Precision.

Aviation administration is rarely one simple task. Documentation, permits, renewals, maintenance coordination, authorities, airports, vendors and operational requirements can involve multiple parties and deadlines.

AVA provides structured administrative coordination so aviation matters are organised, documented and followed through properly.

One point of contact. Multiple moving parts. One coordinated process.

Credibility

Built From Aviation Operations

AVA’s aviation services are informed by first-hand experience working within Thailand’s aviation environment.

The founder previously served as Administrative Manager for aviation administration and operations within a flying-school environment in Thailand, working with international clients and coordinating documentation, scheduling, aircraft and pilot records, maintenance requirements and matters involving relevant Thai aviation authorities.

That experience established the standard AVA applies today: precision, documentation discipline, clear communication and consistent follow-through.

Clear Boundaries Matter in Aviation

AVA provides administrative and coordination support.

We do not provide aviation legal opinions, engineering certification, aircraft maintenance certification, flight-operation decisions or other regulated specialist services.

Where specialist expertise is required, we coordinate with the appropriate qualified organisation or professional.

AVA does not guarantee regulatory approvals, permits or other outcomes controlled by third parties or authorities.
