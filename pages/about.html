About AVA

Independent client-side coordination, local execution and Thailand intelligence.

AVA Executive Services is operated as a client-side coordination and execution company. We do not compete by imitating a generic law firm, visa agent or tour operator. Our role is to protect the client’s interests first, coordinate the right regulated professionals when needed, and provide reliable work on the ground.

What AVA is

The client’s local operating layer. You may still need a lawyer, accountant, tax adviser, translator, immigration professional, bank, property lawyer or aviation specialist. AVA keeps the engagement organised around your objective, ensures the correct professional is engaged, coordinates evidence and administration, maintains the case record, executes agreed local tasks, and reports progress.

What AVA is not

  • Not a substitute for a Thai lawyer’s legal opinion
  • Not a tax adviser unless appropriately licensed and engaged
  • Not a government decision-maker and never a seller of guaranteed approvals
  • Not a nominee or shareholder provider, and never a facilitator of circumvention
  • Not a hidden referral-fee marketplace

Integrity standard

AVA maintains a zero-tolerance policy on nominee arrangements, fabricated documents, and any structure designed to circumvent foreign-ownership restrictions. Fees for AVA coordination, government charges and regulated professional advice are kept separate and transparent. Material risk is escalated, never concealed.

Who we serve

Foreign residents, executives, entrepreneurs, business owners, international families, property owners and investors, digital professionals, aviation clients, and private high-value clients who require discreet, accountable support in Thailand.
