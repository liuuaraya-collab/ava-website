AVA Executive Services is an independent private office providing client-side coordination, executive support and practical services in Thailand.

Credibility

Built on Experience

The foundation behind AVA

AVA was founded by a Thai professional with a background as an Administrative Manager in aviation operations, and several years of experience working within an international aviation environment in Thailand.

Before establishing AVA, the founder served as Administrative Manager responsible for the administration and day-to-day coordination of a flying-school and aviation operation serving international clients.

That role involved scheduling, documentation, aircraft and pilot records, maintenance coordination, client communication, operational administration and matters involving relevant Thai authorities.

Working in aviation requires precision, accountability and respect for procedure. Small details can have significant consequences, and matters often need to be coordinated between multiple people and organisations.

That experience became the foundation for AVA.

Why AVA Exists

People living, working or investing in Thailand often encounter situations that are difficult not because the solution is impossible, but because the process is unfamiliar.

They may not know which department to contact, what documents are required, which professional should be involved or how several parts of a matter connect.

AVA was created to provide that missing layer of coordination. Bring us the situation. We help clarify what needs to happen, identify the appropriate path and coordinate the people involved.
